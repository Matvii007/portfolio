ABOUT

Most SaaS products are hard to show. The interesting part happens between tools, or inside a database, or in a decision the software made while nobody was watching.

A screen recording can’t carry that. A list of features can’t either.

My job is to find the one story that makes the product obvious, and build it — script, storyboard, every screen, the animation, the voice, the sound. You get one file that works on the landing page, in a demo and on socials.

I work remotely from Prešov, Slovakia (CET), in English, Ukrainian or Russian.

WORK

Loupe

CONCEPT · 1:02

An invented claims engine for car insurers. A claim usually takes thirty days because someone has to come and look at the car. Here nobody comes. From six photos on the driver’s phone, Loupe finds every damaged part, down to a bracket you can’t see, names each one by part number, prices the repair at three nearby shops, books the first free slot with the headlamp already on the shelf, and checks the policy and fraud before paying out. A step timer runs in the corner the whole time and stops at eight minutes, while the hazard lights are still on.

AI Floor Manager

CLIENT · POLYNOME, DUBAI · 0:52 · SEAMLESS MIDDLE EAST

Polynome’s AI watches the cameras a store or warehouse already has and turns what it sees into jobs for the staff. The video is built on camera footage: a till closed while the queue grows, a customer walking out, a pallet blocking an aisle. Each one becomes a task on the right employee’s phone and gets fixed before anyone complains. Made for Polynome’s stand at Seamless Middle East in Dubai.

Kilter

CONCEPT · 0:40

An invented AI tool that closes the month for finance teams. Forty thousand bank and ledger lines tangle across the frame, then pull into two glowing bars that snap level — that is the mark. From there the product reconciles March live, answers why cash dropped, and twelve days of closing turn into two. The 3D mark was built in Blender, and every sound in the video was designed from scratch.

HOW IT GOES

  1. 01

    You send the product

    A link and one sentence about who has to understand it. I tell you whether a video is the right thing here, and what it would take. No charge for that part.

  2. 02

    Script and storyboard

    Every frame drawn before a single layer moves, with the words that play over it and the timecode. You see the whole video on one page and change it while changing it is cheap.

  3. 03

    Animation

    Real interface, real numbers, real copy — never grey placeholder bars. Two rounds of revisions are part of it.

  4. 04

    Voice, sound, delivery

    Mixed and mastered to broadcast loudness. You get the master as MP4 + WebM. Vertical cuts and silent loops for feature sections on request.
