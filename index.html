01 Work · product videos

01 — Pitch · Nysanex · 0:57

Nysanex

Storyboard · UI · 3D · Animation · Sound

Nysanex is software for safety inspectors on construction and industrial sites. The video opens on an isometric site: a worker without a helmet next to an excavator, an open edge with no railing. The inspector writes it up on paper, the forms pile up and get lost, and then the same sheets turn into the product: a violation report filled in by AI from one line of description, a contractor checked before anyone lets them on site, a report downloaded in one click. Made in Russian for Nysanex’s brief, with their interface rebuilt screen by screen.

02 — Client · Polynome, Dubai · 0:52 · Seamless Middle East

AI Floor Manager

Script · Storyboard · UI · Animation · Voice · Sound

Polynome’s AI watches the cameras a store or warehouse already has and turns what it sees into jobs for the staff. The video is built on camera footage: a till closed while the queue grows, a customer walking out, a pallet blocking an aisle. Each one becomes a task on the right employee’s phone and gets fixed before anyone complains. Made for Polynome’s stand at Seamless Middle East in Dubai.

03 — Concept · 0:57

Onward

Concept · Script · UI · 3D · Animation · Voice · Sound

An invented rebooking engine for airlines. A flight is cancelled and three hundred passengers start forming a line at one desk. The video asks what if that line never formed: Onward reads every passenger at once, works out who has to be where by when, searches every flight out tonight and puts each person on a new seat before they reach the desk. It ends on a boarding pass already on the phone.

04 — Concept · 0:56

Runtime

Script · Storyboard · UI · Animation · Voice · Sound

A concept video for an AI agent platform. A customer says they were charged twice. The video follows that one complaint all the way down to the line in billing/worker.ts that caused it — and finds it wasn’t one customer, it was twelve. One incident traced end to end, no feature list anywhere.

02 About

Most SaaS products are hard to show.

The interesting part happens between tools, or inside a database, or in a decision the software made while nobody was watching. A screen recording can’t carry that. A list of features can’t either.

My job is to find the one story that makes the product obvious, and build it — script, storyboard, every screen, the animation, the voice, the sound. You get one file that works on the landing page, in a demo and on socials.

I work remotely from Prešov, Slovakia (CET), in English, Ukrainian or Russian.

03 How it goes
  1. 01

    You send the product

    A link and one sentence about who has to understand it. I tell you whether a video is the right thing here, and what it would take. No charge for that part.

  2. 02

    Script and storyboard

    Every frame drawn before a single layer moves, with the words that play over it and the timecode. You see the whole video on one page and change it while changing it is cheap.

  3. 03

    Animation

    Real interface, real numbers, real copy — never grey placeholder bars. Two rounds of revisions are part of it.

  4. 04

    Voice, sound, delivery

    Mixed and mastered to broadcast loudness. You get the master as MP4 + WebM. Vertical cuts and silent loops for feature sections on request.
