Runtime

A product video for a platform that builds agents which use your existing tools.

Details

  • TypeConcept piece — self-initiated
  • Length56 seconds
  • Year2026
  • RoleEverything — script, storyboard, UI, animation, voice, sound

The problem

Runtime is close to invisible. It works across Slack, Zendesk, Stripe, Postgres and a codebase, and everything interesting happens between those tools — never inside any one of them.

A screen recording shows one window at a time, so it shows the least interesting part. A feature list explains what the product has, not what it changes. Both are the wrong shape for this.

The approach

One story instead of a feature tour. A customer says they were charged twice. The agent opens a session and traces the charge — the support ticket, the payment log, the database, the billing code — finds the bug, and stops to ask for approval before moving money.

Then the turn: it wasn't one customer. It was twelve. Nobody had asked the agent to look.

A single line runs through the entire video, and every system the agent touches is a station on it. The viewer never loses the thread, so the payoff lands — they watched it happen rather than being told about it.

What went into it

  • StoryScript and voice direction, written for 56 seconds
  • Design14-scene storyboard and every screen in the film, built in Figma
  • 3DPhone with a live screen, lit and animated in the scene
  • MotionAnimation and compositing in After Effects
  • SoundSound design and final mix at broadcast loudness
