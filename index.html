About

Most SaaS products are hard to show. The interesting part happens between tools, or inside a database, or in a decision the software made while nobody was watching. A screen recording can't carry that. A list of features can't either.

My job is to find the one story that makes the product obvious, and build it — script, storyboard, every screen, the animation, the voice, the sound. You get one file that works on the landing page, in a demo, and on socials.

I work from Prešov, Slovakia, remotely, with teams across Europe and the US.

What I do

  • 01

    Product videos

    The main video for a landing page, a launch, or a raise. Length follows the story and where it runs — a fifteen-second cut for social, a minute on the homepage, several minutes for a walkthrough.
  • 02

    Landing page loops

    Short silent animations for feature sections. Built to sit under 100 KB so they don't cost you page speed.
  • 03

    The whole thing, not a piece of it

    Script, storyboard, UI design, animation, voiceover, sound design, final mix.
