01 How a film is made
  1. 01

    Idea and script

    One rule the whole film plays out, written as scenes, beats and lines — with the product as the turn, not an insert.

  2. 02

    Previs in Blender

    Every shot blocked in grey: lenses, camera moves, positions and timing. The edit is decided before anything is generated.

  3. 03

    Keyframes and generation

    Reference sheets for the cast and world, one approved keyframe per shot, then one take per scene guided by the previs.

  4. 04

    Edit, grade, sound

    After Effects edit, 4K upscale, grade, recast voices, score, ambience and effects — mixed to −14 LUFS.
